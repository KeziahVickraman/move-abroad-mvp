# Neighborhood Guide: Tiong Bahru, Joo Chiat, Tanjong Pagar & Queenstown

Finding the right residential neighborhood is one of the most critical decisions for expats and international students settling in Singapore.

## 1. Tiong Bahru (Art Deco & Heritage Charm)
- **The Vibe:** Low-rise Art Deco flats, spiral staircases, indie bookstores, and artisanal bakeries nestled next to one of Singapore’s best wet markets and hawker centres.
- **Connectivity:** 5 mins bus to Outram Park / Havelock MRT (Thomson-East Coast Line).
- **Ideal For:** Designers, writers, foodies, and couples seeking character and quiet charm.

## 2. Joo Chiat & Katong (East Coast Ease & Shophouses)
- **The Vibe:** Pastel Peranakan shophouses, artisanal specialty cafes, gelato parlors, yoga studios, and cycling access to East Coast Park beach.
- **Connectivity:** Marine Parade MRT (TEL) and Dakota MRT (Circle Line).
- **Ideal For:** Outdoor enthusiasts, pet owners, and expats who prefer a breezy coastal lifestyle.

## 3. Tanjong Pagar & Duxton (Downtown Energy & CBD Proximity)
- **The Vibe:** High-energy shophouse conservation district adjacent to the Central Business District. Home to award-winning cocktail bars, Korean dining row, and fitness studios.
- **Connectivity:** Tanjong Pagar MRT (EWL) and Maxwell MRT (TEL).
- **Ideal For:** Tech founders, finance expats, and individuals who want a short walk to work.

## 4. Queenstown & Dawson (Green Corridors & Satellite Ease)
- **The Vibe:** Lush, peaceful modern residential estates directly connected to the historic Rail Corridor nature parkway.
- **Connectivity:** Queenstown MRT (EWL) to Raffles Place in 12 mins.
- **Ideal For:** Professionals and students seeking convenience, value, and immediate running trail access.
