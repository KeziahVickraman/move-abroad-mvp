# The Multi-Cultural Festival Calendar of Singapore

Singapore’s calendar reflects its rich multi-ethnic heritage. Each community's celebrations are shared nationally as public holidays and neighborhood festivals.

## 1. Lunar New Year (January / February)
Chinatown comes alive with illuminated lanterns, street markets selling festive goodies, and lion dance performances.
- **Key Ritual:** Lo Hei / Yu Sheng (prosperity tossed raw fish salad where diners chant auspicious blessings while tossing ingredients high into the air).
- **Custom:** Exchanging pairs of fresh mandarin oranges when visiting friends and relatives.

## 2. Thaipusam & Deepavali (Tamil & Hindu Heritage)
- **Thaipusam (Jan/Feb):** A deeply spiritual foot procession where devotees carry ornate kavadi structures and milk pots from Sri Srinivasa Perumal Temple to Tank Road.
- **Deepavali (Oct/Nov):** The Festival of Lights transforms Little India with glittering archways, vibrant rangoli floral art, festive sweet bazaars, and gold jewelry stalls.

## 3. Hari Raya Puasa & Ramadan Bazaars (March / April)
The Geylang Serai and Kampong Gelam night bazaars become bustling culinary hubs featuring ramly burgers, dendeng, churros, and traditional Malay textiles.
- **Open House Tradition:** Families welcome friends and neighbors to enjoy rendang, ketupat, and kueh lapis.

## 4. National Day (August 9th)
Singapore celebrates independence with the National Day Parade (NDP) featuring the Red Lions parachutists, mobile columns, military flypasts, and dazzling fireworks over Marina Bay.
