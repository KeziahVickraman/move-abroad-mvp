# MRT & Public Transport Norms: The Unwritten Rules

Singapore’s Mass Rapid Transit (MRT) and public bus network is punctual, ultra-clean, air-conditioned, and remarkably efficient. To blend in and travel smoothly, here are the key guidelines.

## 1. Escalator Discipline: Stand on the Left, Walk on the Right
- **Left Side:** Stand still and hold the handrail.
- **Right Side:** Strictly reserved for people walking or rushing to catch their connection.
- If you have luggage or groceries, tuck them in front of you on the left so you don't block the overtaking lane.

## 2. Platform Queueing: Give Way Before Boarding
- Stand behind the yellow line and queued to the sides of train doors (follow the floor arrows).
- Let all alighting passengers exit completely through the center before stepping in.
- Move towards the centre of the train carriage immediately so people behind you can board before the chime sounds.

## 3. The Reserved Seat Dilemma
Every train cabin has designated priority seats marked with contrasting red/blue signs or bold typography:
- They are intended for the elderly, pregnant women, parents with infants, or people with mobility limitations.
- If nobody needs the seat, you may sit, but remain vigilant and proactively stand up as soon as someone in need boards.
- Keep your eyes up rather than getting entirely lost in noise-canceling headphones.

## 4. Strict Prohibitions
- **No Food or Drinks:** No bubble tea, water sips, candy, or snacks inside MRT stations or on trains (fines up to $500).
- **No Durians:** Due to their strong aroma, durians are explicitly banned on all public transport.
- **No Smoking & No Flammable Liquids.**

## 5. Contactless Payment (SimplyGo)
You do not need a specialized transit card! Simply tap your contactless Mastercard, Visa, Apple Pay, Google Wallet, or EZ-Link card directly at the fare gantry when entering and exiting. Remember to use the same device/card for both tap-in and tap-out to avoid maximum fare charges.
